#233 | Constitution of the Server

⚜

Short Version

SERVER 233 CONSTITUTION

★

Articles

SERVER 233 CONSTITUTION

Art.1. Territory & Resources

Attacks on players, tiles, caravans forbidden. Unallied players not protected. Violator compensates or faces military punishment. If stronger, drops shields; victim (or guildmate) retaliates until equal losses. Sabotage = burning + ban.

Art.2. Crown

Rotates weekly 1→2→3 among Top-3 BM. Schedule published 24h prior. On load days, royal alliance manages queue manually.

Art.3. Cities

Distributed by level: T6 = Top-3, T5 = Top-3+4, T4 = Top-3+4-7, T3 = Top-10, T2 = Top-14, T1 = all KvK participants. No more than one T6 per alliance. Ownership changes by council decision for inactivity, ranking loss, subversion, or peace violation. Two-week ranking drop = ownership review.

Art.4. Governance

Strategic decisions in R5/R4 chat. Recruitment from Top-5 forbidden. Transfers require R5 agreement. Poaching via conflict banned. Each alliance max 2 reps.

Art.5. KvK

Crown holder commands. If unable, another Top-3 takes over. Inaction = loss of priority.

Art.6. Disputes

Settled at leaders’ council.

Art.7. Discipline

Leaving alliance to attack = ban, blacklist, burning. Duration set by Top-3.

Art.8. Toxicity

Insults, provocation, lies forbidden. Mandatory for all.

Long Version

SERVER 233 CONSTITUTION

★

Articles

FULL SERVER CONSTITUTION

Article 1. Territorial Integrity and Resources

1.1. Attacks, looting, and any hostile actions are prohibited against:

· Players of all alliances;

· Resource-gathering tiles on the server;

· Server caravans;

· Server secrets.

1.2. Players without an alliance are not protected.

1.3. The violator must:

· Fully compensate the victim for what was stolen;

· In case of refusal — face military punishment up to burning of troops.

1.4. If the violator is stronger than the injured party:

· The violator must remove all defensive troops from their city walls and drop shields during the period of retaliatory attacks.

· Punishment mechanics: The victim (or their guildmate) strikes the violator’s sanctuary until the violator’s recorded troop losses equal the victim’s losses (in BM equivalent).

· During punishment, it is forbidden to use the hospital to heal wounded troops, remove heroes from defense, or otherwise artificially reduce losses.

· Recording: Before and after punishment, both sides take screenshots of stats (BM and losses). Screenshots are published in the council’s public channel.

· For sabotage (deliberately lowering losses, refusing to drop shield, withdrawing troops), punishment applies — complete burning and permanent ban from server clans (by decision of the Top-5 Council).

1.4.1. Attack order:

· The victim has priority right to deal retaliatory damage.

· The victim’s guildmate (same alliance) if the victim cannot retaliate due to power difference or being offline.

· If neither the victim nor their alliance can retaliate (due to critical power gap), punishment is carried out by the combined forces of the Top-3 alliances.

★

Article 2. Crown Ownership

2.1. The server crown is transferred weekly in rotation among the alliances ranked 1st, 2nd, and 3rd by BM results of the previous week.

2.2. The schedule of crown rotation is approved by the Top-3 council and published in the general channel 24 hours before the change.

2.2.1. Crown rotation cycle: 1 → 2 → 3 → 1. The cycle repeats.

2.3. The royal alliance must, on high-load days for ministers (e.g., “construction” Tuesdays), switch minister appointments and management to manual mode, personally control the queue, and promptly resolve all issues.

★

Article 3. City Distribution

3.1. All server cities are distributed among alliances and KvK participants according to their activity and ranking.

3.2. Distribution scheme:

· Level 6 cities (3 total) go to alliances ranked 1st, 2nd, 3rd.

· Level 5 cities (4 total) go to Top-3 plus 4th place.

· Level 4 cities (7 total) go to Top-3 plus 4th–7th place.

· Level 3 cities (12 total) go to Top-10 alliances.

· Level 2 cities (22 total) go to Top-14 alliances.

· Level 1 cities (38 total) go to all alliances that participated in KvK.

Note: An alliance cannot own more than one Level 6 city. If a Top-3 loses ranking, it must transfer the city to the alliance that rose in rank (per §3.4).

3.3. Decision on city ownership change is made by the general council of alliances.

3.3.1. Grounds for city ownership change:

1. Alliance shows no KvK activity — loses priority in city distribution.

2. Alliance lost its ranking position (see §3.4).

3. Alliance members engaged in subversive activity against the server: spreading rumors, false facts, harassment, target pointing, collusion with other servers.

4. Violation of peaceful coexistence on Server 233: stealing secret operations, attacking caravans or resource tiles.

3.4. Mechanics of city ownership change upon ranking loss:

· If an alliance remains below its qualifying rank for two weeks, the alliance that rose in rank gains the right to apply for ownership review. Applications are accepted one week after the new ranking is set.

★

Article 4. Server Governance and Decision-Making

4.1. All strategic decisions (war declarations, alliances, major reshuffles) are made by the alliance council (R5 and R4) in the active “R5/R4 #233” chat, not in private messages.

1. Direct recruitment of players in Top-5 alliances is prohibited. Violation = warning; repeat = rank demotion.

2. Official transfers: If a Top-5 player wishes to move, transfer must be agreed between both alliances’ R5. The player must leave the old alliance before joining the new one.

3. Poaching via conflict is prohibited: bribery, blackmail, or stirring internal conflict to lure players. Punishment = server blacklist.

4. Note: Players ranked Top-6 and below may freely receive offers but must politely decline if uninterested. Complaints about persistent recruitment go to diplomats.

4.3. For effective communication, each alliance may delegate no more than two representatives (usually R5, R4-diplomat). They are the official voices in councils and negotiations.

Important: Replacement representatives automatically substitute the absent one, but total representatives per alliance must not exceed two. Extra participants must be removed before discussions begin.

★

Article 5. KvK Coordination

5.1. Overall KvK command belongs to the alliance holding the crown that week.

5.2. If circumstances prevent the royal alliance from fulfilling commander duties, command passes to any other Top-3 alliance willing to assume responsibility.

5.3. The commander alliance must:

· Develop overall KvK strategy;

· Coordinate plan with Top-3 alliances;

· Ensure coordination of all participating alliances.

5.4. Alliances may:

· Propose strategy adjustments via representatives;

· Request plan review at commander council.

5.5. All alliances must participate in KvK. Inaction = loss of priority in next city/resource distribution.

★

Article 6. Dispute Resolution

6.1. Disputes over city and resource ownership are resolved at the leaders’ council.

Article 7. Discipline and Responsibility

7.1. A player who deliberately leaves their alliance to commit hostile actions against another alliance:

· loses right to join any server alliance;

· Is blacklisted;

· Subject to burning and resource block (loss of minister posts if caught in queue).

7.2. Punishment duration is determined by the server’s Top-3 alliances.

★

Article 8. Ending Destructive Behavior

8.1. Prohibited for all players, regardless of alliance:

· Insults of any kind in server chats;

· Provocations and incitement to conflict;

· Spreading false information (disinformation, rumors, slander);

· Any actions creating a toxic atmosphere.

8.2. This requirement is mandatory for everyone, from regular players to top alliance leaders.

8.3. Enforcement:

· First violation recorded by alliance council. Warning issued by R5 or diplomat with explanation of rules.

· Second violation (repeat destructive behavior) = disqualification from current alliance and blacklisting for a term set by the alliance council.

Sanctuary

The Sanctuary is the main building in Last Asylum Plague. Upgrading it helps you progress in the game and unlock new features, including upgrades for other buildings. It also increases your Might, Hero Level Cap, Hero HP, Hero ATK, and Hero DEF.

To upgrade the Sanctuary, you first need to upgrade some required buildings and also gather stars and resources. The Lab is always required after Sanctuary level 10, while the other requirements can rotate between different buildings.

Upgrade Requirements

Select a Sanctuary level to view its base resources, requirements, construction times, and unlocks. The actual time and cost you see in the game may be slightly lower because they depend on several factors, such as your Builder’s Hut level, alliance research, help from alliance members, survivors, and the level 6 Noble Fortress.

Alliance Duel

The Alliance Duel is a six-day event in Last Asylum Plague where two alliances compete to earn the most points by performing different daily tasks. The event starts on Monday and ends on Saturday. To take part in the event, your alliance must be ranked in the top 32 alliances on the server before matchmaking begins.

The event has six stages: Raven, Construction, Tech, Hero, Preparation, and Raid. From Monday to Saturday, a new stage starts each day. The sixth stage, called Raid, is where you earn points by attacking members of the opposing alliance.

Daily points reset at 00:00 UTC. The alliance that earns more points for the day gets more Total Points. Each stage gives a different amount of winning points, and the alliance with the most winning points at the end of the event wins. Members of the winning alliance receive better rewards, while the losing alliance still gets rewards but not as good.

There are also daily and weekly leaderboards where players can earn extra rewards by placing high on the rankings.

Tap a card to see the tasks and points of each phase. Tap the title bar to flip it back.

How to Request a Minister and What Do They Do?

The most important thing to remember is: get the Minister title first, then start the action.

For example, if you want to build something faster:

Request Minister of Construction → Receive the title → Start the construction.

Don't start the construction first and then request the title, because the bonus won't apply retroactively.

Also, having VIP active can further reduce your construction costs, so make sure to take advantage of it when you're planning major upgrades.

Get the appropriate Minister title → Start the research/training → Enjoy the bonus.

Elixir Scramble

Registration

⚜

To sign up, select “Select Participants.” This is where you can view the available time slots and choose the one you want to participate in.

Objective

⚜

The goal is to capture and hold buildings to earn points. After 30 minutes, the team with the most points wins.

Participation

⚜

20 starters + 10 substitutes.

Starters have priority.

Once registration closes, the participant list is locked.

Matchmaking

⚜

Matchmaking is based on the total power of the participants.

The game uses each player's highest historical power when determining the matchup.

Preparation — 5 Minutes Before

⚜

Starters can enter the battlefield early to get into position.

During this preparation period, players are placed in a safe zone and cannot be attacked.

Battle Phases

⚜

★ Start

The following buildings are available at the beginning:

➜ Alchemy Workshop

➜ Migration Portal

➜ Observatory

➜ Healing Shops

★ Minute 10

The main objectives become available:

➜ Elixir Castle

➜ War Relics → +15% team buff

➜ Cursed Altar → -15% enemy debuff

★ Key Buildings

➜ Migration Portal: provides +50% mobility.

➜ Healing Shops: help sustain your troops and keep your marches fighting.

➜ Observatory: provides +10% points. Securing it early is a major advantage.

➜ Alchemy Workshop: provides a large amount of points.

➜ War Relics: provide a team-wide buff.

➜ Cursed Altar: applies a debuff to the enemy team.

➜ Elixir Castle: the most important objective and the building that can decide the entire match.

★ Secondary Objectives

➜ Medkits: provide additional points.

➜ Camps: provide relatively few points, so they are lower priority.

★ Keys to Winning

➜ Map control > winning individual fights.

➜ Fast rotations > sitting on one building for too long.

➜ Early Observatory control = a major advantage.

➜ Elixir Castle = absolute priority.

➜ Use your buffs and debuffs at the right time to gain an advantage over the enemy.
